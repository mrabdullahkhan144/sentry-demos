# SENTRY — Lahore outreach playbook

Ten demos are built. Ten phone numbers are below. Your entire job is sending messages.

---

## Before you send anything

1. Open `generate.py`, line 24. Already done — your number is wired in.
2. Skip this step.
3. Host `demos/` anywhere free — Netlify Drop, GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Pages. Drag the
   folder in, you get a URL in about 40 seconds.
4. Open one demo on your own phone first. If it looks broken on your phone, it looks
   broken on theirs.

---

## Message 1 — the send

Send between 11am and 1pm, or 7pm and 9pm. Not Friday afternoon.

> Assalam-o-alaikum. I'm Abdullah, I build AI assistants for businesses here in Lahore.
>
> I made a working one for [BUSINESS NAME] to show you what it does — it's live here:
> [LINK]
>
> Open it and ask it anything: your timings, your services, prices. It answers using your
> real information.
>
> I built it before asking because talking about it is useless. Have a look, and if it's
> not for you, no problem at all.

Three things make this work: their name in it, a link that already works, and an exit.
Do not add anything else. Do not send a voice note. Do not send five paragraphs.

## Follow-up 1 — after 48 hours, only if no reply

> Just checking you saw this — [LINK]
>
> The main thing it does is catch the people who message you at 11pm and get no answer

## Follow-up 2 — day six, then stop

> I'll leave this here. If it's ever useful, the link stays up: [LINK]
>
> Best of luck with the [gym / clinic / salon].

Two follow-ups. Then move on. Chasing is how you burn a list.

---

## When they reply "how much"

Never answer with a number alone. Answer with a question first.

> Depends on whether you want it just on your website, or on your WhatsApp too. Which
> would actually help you more?

Then quote:

| | Setup (one-time) | Monthly |
|---|---|---|
| Website only | Rs 12,000 | Rs 4,000 |
| Website + WhatsApp | Rs 20,000 | Rs 7,000 |

**First three clients:** halve the setup in exchange for a video testimonial and
permission to use their name. Say that out loud — it makes the discount feel earned
rather than desperate, and the testimonial is worth more than the cash.

The monthly is the whole business. Ten clients on Rs 7,000 is Rs 70,000 a month that
arrives whether you work or not. That is the number to keep in your head.

## Objections

**"We already have staff for this."**
Your staff sleep. Ask them how many WhatsApp messages are sitting unread from last
night. This handles those, and passes the serious ones to your staff with a name and
number attached.

**"How do I know it won't say something wrong?"**
It only answers from information you give me. Ask the demo a price question — it
refuses to guess and offers to connect you instead. That's deliberate.

**"Send me details on email."**
That's usually a no. Answer once, briefly, then stop: *"Everything's in the link — try
asking it a few questions, that explains it better than anything I could write."*

**"Too expensive."**
What does one new member/patient/client bring you in a year? If this catches one extra
per month, it has paid for itself several times. If it catches none in the first month,
I'll refund it.

Offer that refund. You will almost never pay it, and it removes the risk from their
side of the table entirely.

## Adding more targets

Open `businesses.json`, copy a block, fill in the fields, run `generate.py` again.
Two minutes per business. The knowledge base builds itself from the profile.

Keep every fact truthful and public — hours, address, staff names from their own page.
The moment you invent a price, the demo becomes a liability instead of a gift.

Ten messages sent. Expect two to four replies. Expect one to want a call.
One paying client at Rs 20,000 + Rs 7,000/month is a real business with one customer.
